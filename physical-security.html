Physical & Social Engineering Security

Security isn't just about technology — it's also about people and physical spaces. Small actions every day help protect our people, information, equipment, and facilities.

7 Simple Habits That Keep Us Safe

1
🚪
Don't tailgate through secure doors
Never let unbadged people follow you into secure areas. If someone is waiting behind you, close the door and ask them to show their badge.
💡 TIP
One person holding the door open for someone else can put everyone at risk.
2
🔌
Never plug in unknown USB drives
USB drives from unknown sources can carry malware that can harm our systems and steal sensitive company data.
💡 TIP
If you find a USB drive, report it to IT immediately. Don't plug it in.
3
👀
Be aware of shoulder surfing
Others may try to look at your screen or watch you enter passwords, PINs, or other sensitive corporate information.
💡 TIP
Position your screen away from others and use privacy screen filters where possible.
4
🪪
Don't share your access badge
Your badge is for you only. Never lend it to anyone or let others "borrow" it, even for a short time.
💡 TIP
Sharing badges can lead to unauthorized access and serious security consequences.
5
💻
Practice clean desk habits
Don't leave sensitive documents, devices, or printed material unattended. Lock computer screens (Win + L) whenever you step away.
💡 TIP
A clean desk keeps information safe and reduces the risk of loss or theft.
6
👥
Verify before you let anyone in
Always verify the identity of visitors, contractors, and delivery personnel before granting access — especially in factory and production areas.
💡 TIP
Ask for ID, confirm their purpose, and strictly follow site visitor escort procedures.
7
📋
Report lost or stolen badges immediately
Report a lost or stolen badge right away so it can be deactivated and unauthorized facility access prevented immediately.
💡 TIP
The sooner you report it, the sooner we can protect our site, equipment, and people.
