Use a 4-word random passphrase of at least 16 characters (e.g. Coffee#Laptop!Sunset9). Long passphrases are exponentially harder for brute-force attacks to crack compared to short complex passwords.
💡 Best Practice: Never reuse your corporate Midas password on personal sites like Facebook, Netflix, or personal banking. Test your passphrase using our Passphrase Strength Evaluator.
