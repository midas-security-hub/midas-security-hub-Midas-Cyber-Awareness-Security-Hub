Key Security Tips

Follow these best practices to keep your remote work and Wi-Fi usage safe and secure.

1

Use Secure Wi-Fi Networks

Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive work. Use your home or company network, or a trusted VPN when you must use public Wi-Fi.

💡 Tip: Look for "https" in website addresses and avoid unsecured networks.
2

Enable a VPN

A Virtual Private Network (VPN) encrypts your internet connection, keeping your data private and safe from eavesdroppers.

💡 Tip: Always connect to the company VPN when working remotely.
3

Keep Your Devices Secure

Use strong passwords, enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) and keep your operating system and apps up to date.

💡 Tip: Turn on automatic updates whenever possible.
4

Secure Your Home Wi-Fi

Use a strong, unique password for your Wi-Fi router, enable WPA2/WPA3 encryption and keep your router firmware updated.

💡 Tip: Avoid using default router credentials (e.g. admin/admin).
5

Be Aware of Phishing

Cyber attackers may target you through fake emails, messages or websites, especially when you're working remotely.

💡 Tip: Verify links, check sender details and never share your credentials.
6

Protect Company Data

Avoid sharing sensitive information over insecure networks. Don't store work files on personal devices or untrusted cloud services.

💡 Tip: Use approved tools and follow data handling policies.
