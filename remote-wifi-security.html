🛡️ 6 Golden Rules of Remote & Wi-Fi Security

Essential remote working protocols to prevent data leaks while on the move.

01

Always Use Midas Corporate VPN

Turn on corporate VPN whenever accessing internal emails, SharePoint, ERP, or client data outside office locations.

02

Disable Auto-Connect to Wi-Fi

Disable "Auto-Connect to Open Wi-Fi" on work laptops and mobile devices to prevent automatically joining rogue networks.

03

Beware of Evil-Twin Hotspots

Attackers deploy fake access points named "Starbucks_Guest" or "Airport_Free_WiFi". Always verify exact network names with staff.

04

Prevent Shoulder Surfing

Use privacy screen filters in airports, cafes, and trains to block unauthorized viewing of confidential corporate screens.

05

Never Leave Assets Unattended

Lock laptop screens (Win + L) instantly and never leave devices unattended in hotel lobbies, vehicles, or coffee shops.

06

Use Personal Mobile Tethering

When trusted Wi-Fi is unavailable, tether your laptop to your smartphone's WPA3 mobile hotspot instead of untrusted open networks.
