Account & Password Security

Strong accounts keep our people, data, and operations safe.

Follow these 9 simple steps to protect your accounts and prevent unauthorized access. Small habits today stop big problems tomorrow.

The 9 Essential Steps for Account Security

Implement these practices to keep company and personal credentials safe.

01
Use Strong, Unique Passphrases

Combine 4 or more random words (e.g., coffee-ocean-rocket-guitar) to create long, memorable passphrases. Never reuse passwords across work and personal accounts.

💡 Tip: Aim for 16+ characters minimum
02
Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA adds a critical second layer of protection. Always approve push notifications from trusted authenticator apps (e.g. Microsoft Authenticator) and reject unexpected prompts.

🛡️ MFA blocks over 99% of automated attacks
03
Never Share Credentials or MFA Codes

IT Support will never ask for your password or one-time verification code. Never send credentials via email, Teams chat, or phone.

⛔ Keep passcodes 100% confidential
04
Use an Approved Password Manager

Store complex passwords in an encrypted vault. Avoid writing passwords on sticky notes, notebooks, or unencrypted text documents on your desk.

🔑 Vaults auto-fill & detect phishing domains
05
Watch Out for Phishing & Suspicious Links

Be suspicious of unexpected urgent password reset requests, strange login screens, or unusual emails asking you to click links. Always check the sender's full address.

🔍 Hover over links before clicking
06
Lock Screens & Protect Devices

Always lock your workstation (press Windows + L or Cmd + Control + Q) whenever you leave your desk, whether at the office, home, or cafe.

💻 Win + L is your quick lock habit
07
Report Incidents & Suspicious Activity Promptly

If you accidentally click a suspicious link, enter credentials on a phishing page, or approve an unexpected prompt, inform Information Security immediately.

⚡ Quick reporting limits potential damage
08
Keep Software & Operating Systems Updated

Regularly install OS, browser, and app security updates. Software patches close security vulnerabilities before hackers can exploit them.

🔄 Enable automatic background updates
09
Audit Active Sessions & Connected Apps

Periodically review active login sessions, third-party app permissions, and recovery options. Revoke access for unused devices or unknown applications.

⚙️ Perform quarterly account checkups
