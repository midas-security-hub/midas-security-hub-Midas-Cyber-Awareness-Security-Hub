Device Security

Follow these simple steps to protect your company data and keep your devices safe every day across Windows, Android, and iOS platforms.

Jump to section:
💡 Not sure what you use? Check your device settings or ask your IT manager.

Windows Device Security

Simple steps to help you keep your Windows laptop or desktop and company data safe.

Section 1: Windows
1
Lock your computer when leaving
Press Windows + L every time you step away from your desk or workstation.
💡 TIP Set automatic screen lock timeout to 5 minutes or less in Windows Settings.
2
Keep BitLocker & Windows Defender Active
Ensure full-disk BitLocker encryption and real-time antivirus protection remain enabled.
💡 TIP Never disable Windows Defender for unverified software or file downloads.
3
Install Windows Updates & Patches Promptly
Software updates fix security vulnerabilities before malicious actors can exploit them.
💡 TIP Restart your computer weekly to allow pending system updates to complete.
4
Use Approved Software & Password Vaults
Only install apps approved by IT Security and store work credentials in password managers.
💡 TIP Avoid saving corporate logins in plain browser password stores.
5
Secure Remote Work & VPN Connections
Always activate corporate VPN when working remotely or connecting to public Wi-Fi networks.
💡 TIP Never use open airport or hotel Wi-Fi without active VPN encryption.
6
Watch Out for Malicious Attachments
Do not open unverified .exe, .vbs, or macro-enabled documents from unexpected emails.
💡 TIP Inspect the sender domain before opening attached files.
7
Report Lost PC or Unusual Behavior Immediately
Contact IT Helpdesk immediately if your computer is missing, stolen, or behaving strangely.
💡 TIP Quick reporting allows IT Security to isolate threats and protect credentials.
🛡️ WINDOWS GOLDEN RULE: Never disable Windows Defender, BitLocker, or automatic security patches.

Android Device Security

Simple steps to help you keep your Android phone or tablet and company data safe.

Section 2: Android
1
Keep your device locked
Use a strong PIN, pattern or password. Enable biometric unlock (fingerprint or face) if available.
💡 TIP A 6-digit PIN or longer is much better than a 4-digit PIN.
2
Install apps only from Google Play
Apps from unknown sources or sideloaded APKs can contain malware that steals your data.
💡 TIP If it's not from the Play Store, don't install.
3
Keep Android OS & apps up to date
Updates fix security holes and protect you from the latest mobile threats and exploits.
💡 TIP Turn on "Auto-update" for apps in Google Play Store settings.
4
Enable Find My Device
Allows you to locate, lock, or remotely wipe your Android device if it is lost or stolen.
💡 TIP Check that Location & Find My Device are enabled in Google settings.
5
Use Android Enterprise Work Profile
Isolate work apps and emails from personal data using a managed Android Work Profile.
💡 TIP Keeps corporate files encrypted and separate from personal photos.
6
Avoid Open Wi-Fi without VPN
Never access confidential work tools on unencrypted public Wi-Fi without corporate VPN.
💡 TIP Turn off "Auto-connect to open networks" in Wi-Fi settings.
7
Review App Permissions Regularly
Revoke sensitive location, camera, and microphone permissions for unnecessary apps.
💡 TIP Check "Permission Manager" in Settings to inspect app access.
✅ ANDROID GOLDEN RULE: If it's not from the Play Store, don't install.

iOS Device Security

Simple steps to help you keep your iPhone or iPad and company data safe.

Section 3: iOS
1
Use a strong passcode
Use a 6-digit passcode or longer. Enable Face ID or Touch ID if available.
💡 TIP Avoid easy codes like 123456 or your birthdate.
2
Install apps only from the App Store
Apps from outside the official App Store may be unsafe and can put your data at risk.
💡 TIP If it's not from the App Store, don't install.
3
Keep iOS & apps up to date
Updates include important security fixes and improve overall system protection.
💡 TIP Turn on "Automatic Updates" in Settings -> General -> Software Update.
4
Enable Find My iPhone
Helps you locate, lock, or erase your iPhone or iPad if it is lost or stolen.
💡 TIP Ensure "Send Last Location" is enabled in Find My settings.
5
Hide Sensitive Lock Screen Notifications
Prevent message previews and verification codes from displaying when your screen is locked.
💡 TIP Set Notification Previews to "When Unlocked" in Settings.
6
Enable Encrypted Backups & Data Protection
Protect iCloud backups and local computer backups with strong password encryption.
💡 TIP Ensures corporate app tokens in backups remain safe.
7
Turn off Auto-Join Unverified Wi-Fi
Prevent your iPhone from automatically connecting to rogue or unencrypted Wi-Fi hotspots.
💡 TIP Set "Ask to Join Networks" to Notify or Off in Wi-Fi settings.
✔ iOS GOLDEN RULE: If it's not from the App Store, don't install.
