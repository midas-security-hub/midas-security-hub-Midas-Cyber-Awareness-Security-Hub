OUR AI PRINCIPLES

Six Principles for Responsible AI

These principles guide how we use AI at Midas Safety.

Learn more about the principles

Human Accountability

People remain in control and are accountable for AI use and outcomes.

Responsible & Ethical Use

We use AI in a way that is fair, legal and aligned with our values.

Transparency

We are open about how AI is used and its limitations.

Data Security

We protect our data, customers and intellectual property.

Fairness & Non-Discrimination

We avoid bias and ensure AI benefits everyone fairly.

Continuous Learning

We stay informed, build skills and improve our practices.

RED ZONE

Prohibited Use

Do not use AI for the following:

1
Share confidential, personal or sensitive data.
2
Upload or input Midas IP, trade secrets or proprietary info.
3
Use AI for illegal, unethical or harmful activities.
4
Bypass security controls or attempt to break systems.
5
Use AI to create or share discriminatory or biased content.
6
Represent AI-generated content as your own work.
7
Use unapproved (public) AI tools for company work.
!
When in doubt, don't input it.
Ask your manager, IT or the AI Steering Committee.
